About Us

MKP TRUCKING SERVICE

A forward-looking transportation and logistics service provider dedicated to delivering reliable, efficient, and convenient trucking and moving solutions to meet the business and personal needs of individuals, families, businesses, and commercial establishments.

Our Vision

To become a trusted and preferred trucking and logistics service provider, recognized for dependable transportation solutions, professional customer service, and a commitment to helping individuals and businesses move their belongings and goods efficiently and securely.

Our Mission

To provide accessible, reliable, and customer-focused trucking services that make residential relocation, commercial moving, and goods transportation more convenient and efficient.

Special Equipment

Equipped with Hydraulic Lifters for Secure Cargo Handling

We take pride in pioneering simple, complete, and specialized moving services through the use of our trucks equipped with hydraulic lifters, designed to make loading, unloading, and transporting goods more efficient, convenient, and secure.

Our Services

Comprehensive Trucking & Relocation Solutions

Whether transferring household belongings to a new residence, relocating an entire office, or transporting essential business supplies, we provide tailored transport solutions.

Equipment

Dependable transportation for businesses, retailers, suppliers, and commercial clients requiring general goods and merchandise delivery.

Medical Equipment

Warehouse Transfer Services

Telco Equipment Transport

Business Client Services

Reliable logistics support for construction suppliers and contractors moving building materials to project locations.

Office Relocation

Condominium Relocation

Coordinated moving solutions adhering strictly to building regulations, elevator schedules, and property management rules.

House Moving / Lipat-Bahay

Making family residential relocations stress-free and convenient for personal belongings, furniture, and appliances.
